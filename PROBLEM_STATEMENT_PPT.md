# Problem Statement - Inventory Tracker

## The Challenge for Small Business Owners

**Current Situation:**
Small business owners today struggle with inventory management because existing solutions are overcomplicated, expensive, and difficult to implement. These systems often require:
- Extensive technical training and IT support
- Complex setup and ongoing maintenance
- High licensing costs that strain limited budgets
- Steep learning curves that frustrate staff
- Integration headaches with existing tools

---

## Key Problems Faced

### 1. **Complexity Overload**
Existing Enterprise ERP and inventory systems are built for large corporations with dedicated IT teams. Small businesses don't need 80% of these features, yet are forced to pay for and navigate them.

### 2. **Cost Barriers**
Premium inventory solutions like NetSuite, SAP, or Oracle cost thousands monthly. Small businesses operate on thin margins and cannot justify this expense.

### 3. **Time Wastage**
Manual tracking using spreadsheets leads to:
- Data entry errors and inconsistencies
- Time wasted on repetitive tasks
- Difficulty tracking real-time stock levels
- Delayed decision-making

### 4. **Visibility & Control**
Without a centralized system, small business owners lack:
- Real-time inventory insights
- Order tracking across suppliers and customers
- Employee and warehouse management
- Data-driven decision-making capabilities
- Audit trails for accountability

### 5. **Operational Inefficiencies**
- No integration between stock, orders, suppliers, and payroll
- Manual reconciliation processes
- Inability to identify trends or optimize operations
- Poor communication between teams and departments

---

## The Impact

- **Lost Sales** from stockouts or inaccurate inventory data
- **Increased Costs** from overstocking and wastage
- **Reduced Efficiency** due to manual processes and silos
- **Limited Scalability** as businesses grow
- **Missed Opportunities** for data-driven growth

---

## What's Needed

A simple, affordable, easy-to-use inventory management solution that:
✓ Requires minimal technical expertise  
✓ Costs reasonably for small businesses  
✓ Provides real-time visibility  
✓ Automates core processes  
✓ Integrates key operations (inventory, orders, suppliers, staff, reports)  
✓ Enables data-driven decisions through insights and analytics  
✓ Scales with business growth
